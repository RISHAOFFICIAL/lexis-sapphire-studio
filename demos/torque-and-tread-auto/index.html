Our Story

Three generations on East Jefferson

Torque & Tread opened in 1998 in a two-bay garage on East Jefferson Ave, started by Marcus Delgado with a toolbox, a borrowed lift and one rule: never sell a repair the car does not need. Word traveled the way it does in Detroit, one honest brake job at a time.

Today the shop runs six bays with a crew of nine, including Marcus, his daughter Elena who runs the front counter, and two master techs who have been with us for more than a decade. We still road test every car, still hand back the old parts, and still answer the phone with a person, not a menu.

Good Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment, or can I just walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome for oil changes, tire repairs and battery tests, and we will fit you in the same day whenever a bay is open. For brakes, diagnostics and bigger jobs, an appointment guarantees your spot and lets us have parts ready before you arrive.

How long does a brake job usually take?

Most pad and rotor jobs take 2 to 3 hours once the car is in the bay. If we find a caliper or hose issue during the inspection, we call you with photos and a price before adding anything. Same day pickup is the norm.

What does the 24 month / 24,000 mile warranty cover?

Parts and labor on every repair we perform, honored at more than 17,000 partner shops nationwide. If a part we installed fails within the window, the repair is free. Tires carry their own manufacturer treadwear warranties, listed on each tier above.

Do you work on hybrids and electric vehicles?

Yes. Our techs are trained and equipped for hybrid and EV service, including brakes, tires, suspension, 12-volt batteries and cooling systems. For high-voltage battery work we will tell you honestly whether it is a job for us or for the dealer.

Can I get a ride home while my car is in the shop?

Absolutely. Our free shuttle runs within 5 miles of the shop, and we will pick you up when your car is ready. For repairs that run past one day, ask about a loaner when you book. We keep two on hand and they go fast on Mondays.

Will you tell me if a repair can wait?

That is the whole point of our inspection report. Every item is marked green, yellow or red. Yellow items come with a mileage or time estimate, so you can budget and plan instead of getting surprised at your next oil change.

Big repair? Pay over time.

With TorquePay financing, repairs over $199 can be split into monthly payments with 6 months of no interest for qualified customers. Apply at the counter in about five minutes, most approvals are instant.
