Pre-Planning

Plan today, and spare your family the hardest decisions later.

Pre-planning simply means writing down your wishes ahead of time. There is no cost to meet with us, and no obligation to buy anything.

1

Record your wishes

Burial or cremation, service style, music, readings, and the details that matter to you, kept on file with us and shared with the people you choose.

2

Know the cost in advance

We price your plan line by line so your family is never guessing. You may pre-pay to lock in today's prices, or simply keep the plan on file.

3

Change it anytime

Life changes, and plans can too. Your pre-plan can be updated or transferred at any time with a phone call.

Our Story

Three generations of one Detroit family

Serenity Gardens was founded in 1962 by Harold and Evelyn Whitmore in a converted brick home on Livernois Avenue. Harold believed a funeral home should feel like a neighbor's front room, warm, unhurried, and honest. Evelyn kept a garden behind the chapel where families could sit quietly between services. That garden still blooms every spring.

Today their grandchildren run the funeral home with the same promise: treat every family like our own, explain every cost in plain language, and never rush a goodbye.

Questions

Common questions, answered plainly

What should we do first when a loved one passes?

Call us at (313) 555-0173, any hour. If the death occurs at home, call 911 first so it can be officially pronounced, then call us. If it occurs in a hospital or hospice, the staff will ask which funeral home to contact. Give them our name, and we will take it from there.

How much does a funeral cost?

It depends on the choices you make, and we will show you every price before you decide. Direct cremation starts at $1,295, cremation with a service at $2,495, and a traditional funeral at $4,995. You will receive a written, itemized statement, and you may choose only the items you want.

Can we personalize the service?

Yes, and we encourage it. Families have brought in photo displays, favorite music, a beloved quilt, even a motorcycle parked by the chapel door. If it tells your loved one's story, we will find a way to include it.

Do you serve all faiths and traditions?

Yes. We regularly serve Christian, Muslim, Jewish, and non-religious families, and we work closely with clergy and cultural leaders across Detroit. Tell us what your tradition requires, and we will honor it.

What is the difference between pre-planning and pre-paying?

Pre-planning means recording your wishes with us, which costs nothing. Pre-paying means funding those wishes in advance, which locks in today's prices. Many families pre-plan first and decide about pre-paying later. Either way, your plan can be changed at any time.
