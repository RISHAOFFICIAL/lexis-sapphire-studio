About Keysha

She traded code for comedy.

Keysha E. is a Detroit native who started out as a computer engineer, with degrees in information systems and computer engineering and a masters from Kettering University.

Then a dare from friends landed her on stage doing stand-up, a gift she never even knew she had. She bet on herself, moved to Los Angeles, and never looked back.

Now she performs comedy in Hollywood and acts on screen, and she brings Detroit with her everywhere she goes.
