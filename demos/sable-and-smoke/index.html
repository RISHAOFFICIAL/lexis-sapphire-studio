Our Story

Started at a church kitchen. Still cooking like it.

Sable & Smoke opened in 2016 in a 40-seat storefront on Gratiot, run by husband and wife team Darnell and Tanya Whitfield. Darnell learned to smoke meat from his uncle in Mississippi. Tanya learned to season greens standing on a stool in her grandmother's kitchen in Alabama. The menu is where those two kitchens met.

Our head chef, Marcus Bell, joined in 2019 and runs the line six days a week. Everything is made in house, from the cornbread batter to the hot honey on the wings. The smoker out back runs on oak and hickory, and the ribs go on at 5am so they are ready by lunch.

We seat 64 in the main room and 28 more on the patio when the weather cooperates. Walk-ins are always welcome, and the sweet tea is always cold.

Come hungry. Leave family. (Darnell and Tanya)

Questions

Before you ask.

Do you take reservations?

We seat walk-ins first come, first served. For parties of 6 or more, call (313) 555-0147 and we will hold a table for 15 minutes past your time. Friday and Saturday nights fill up by 6:30pm, so early is smart.

Where do I park?

Our free private lot sits behind the building with the entrance off Gratiot Avenue. There is also street parking on Russell Street. On Eastern Market Saturdays the lot fills by noon, so plan a few extra minutes.

How far ahead should I book catering?

Give us 7 days for drop-off catering and 14 days for staffed events. Holiday weeks (Thanksgiving, Christmas, Mother's Day) book out a month ahead. A 50% deposit holds your date.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. The Garden Plate is our vegetarian entree, and most sides can be made without meat on request, including the greens and red beans. Tell your server and the kitchen will take care of you.

Can I buy a gift card?

Gift cards are available at the counter or by phone in any amount from $10 to $200. They never expire and work for dining room, takeout, and catering deposits.

Is the patio dog friendly?

Leashed dogs are welcome on the patio, April through October. We keep a water bowl by the gate and the kitchen has been known to send out a plain grilled chicken bite for good dogs.
