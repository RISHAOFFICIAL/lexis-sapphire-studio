Our Story

One flat-top. Zero shortcuts.

We opened our counter on Grand River in 2019 with a used flat-top, a stack of fresh rolls, and one rule: chop it fresh, melt it right, hand it over hot. Six years later the rule has not changed, and neither has the line at lunch.

Our ribeye is shaved in house every morning. The rolls come from a Detroit bakery at sunrise. The cheese is Cooper Sharp because that is what a real cheesesteak deserves, and the fries are cut by hand before the doors open.

Pull up a red stool, watch your sandwich come together on the griddle, and see the pull for yourself.
