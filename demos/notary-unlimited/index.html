Good to know before your appointment

A few simple things that help your signing go smoothly.

  • Bring a valid photo ID. A driver license, state ID, or passport that is not expired.
  • Do not sign ahead of time. Your documents must be signed in front of the notary.
  • All signers must be present. Everyone whose signature is being notarized needs to be there in person.
  • Know what you are signing. A notary verifies identity and witnesses signatures. We cannot give legal advice or explain legal documents.
