Our Story

Started at a kitchen table in 2019

Maple and Main started the way a lot of good Detroit things start: at a kitchen table, with a notebook full of recipes and a stubborn belief that this neighborhood deserved a coffee shop of its own. Our founder spent six years baking for family, friends, and every church bake sale on the block before signing the lease on this corner.

We named the shop for the two things that hold the place together: the maple syrup in our signature latte, tapped right here in Michigan, and the main street energy of West McNichols, where everybody still waves from the sidewalk.

Today our little team of eleven baristas and bakers roasts with a Detroit roaster, bakes everything in house, and knows most of our regulars by name and by order. If it is your first visit, do not worry. By your third, we will know yours too.

Questions

Good questions, straight answers

Do you have Wi-Fi, and can I work here all day?

Yes and yes. Wi-Fi is free, outlets are at most tables, and drip coffee refills are on us while you stay. The upstairs corner is our quiet zone for laptops and studying.

Do you offer dairy free and gluten free options?

Every drink can be made with oat, almond, or soy milk. In the bakery case we always have at least two gluten free items, and our bakers keep a separate pan and utensils for them.

Can I order pastries or coffee for my office or event?

Absolutely. Pastry boxes, whole cakes, and coffee to go boxes can be ordered 48 hours ahead by calling (313) 555-0119. We deliver within 5 miles for orders over $50.

How does Maple Rewards work?

Give us your phone number at the register and you earn 1 point per dollar. After 10 drink purchases, your next drink is free. Points never expire, and Tuesdays earn double.

Is the shop kid friendly and dog friendly?

Both. Kids get their own steamer drinks and a small play corner near the register. Dogs are welcome on the patio, and there is always a water bowl by the door.
