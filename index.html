How it works

Every app project follows a stage based process. You approve each stage before we move to the next, so there are no surprises.

  1. 01

    Persona

    Who the app is for and what they need.

  2. 02

    User Stories

    What users do, in their own words.

  3. 03

    User Flow

    How screens connect, end to end.

  4. 04

    Lo Fi

    Rough wireframes to test the structure.

  5. 05

    Hi Fi

    Polished designs with Apple HIG and Liquid Glass.

  6. 06

    Build

    Development in sprints with regular check ins.

About the studio

Lexis Sapphire Studio is an iOS development studio in Detroit building apps with Swift and SwiftUI. The studio graduated from the Apple Developer Academy Foundations Program and holds a Mobile Application Certificate from Wayne County Community College District. The engineering background includes a BS in Industrial Engineering and an MS in Engineering Management from Kettering University.

Lexis Sapphire Studio exists to do focused, high quality work for clients who need apps built right or books kept clean. AI is used as a pair programmer to ship faster, and Apple Human Interface Guidelines are followed on every Apple project.
