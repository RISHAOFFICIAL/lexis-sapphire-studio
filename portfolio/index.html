Coffee Shop App

A Detroit coffee shop directory plus personal visited log, so you never forget the name of a shop you loved months later.

Status: In development. Sprints 1-3 complete: SwiftData models, design system, tab shell, Supabase client, Discover map, search, filters, and check-in with region monitoring.
